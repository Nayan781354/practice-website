About Meghalaya

Meghalaya, meaning "Abode of Clouds", is a paradise of natural beauty and rich heritage. From the world's wettest place to Asia's cleanest village, every corner of Meghalaya tells a unique story.

Home to the living root bridges

Breathtaking waterfalls and turquoise rivers

Vibrant tribal culture and festivals

Warm hospitality and delicious local cuisine

Top Destinations in Meghalaya

Cherrapunji

Home to the world's highest rainfall and stunning waterfalls.

Shillong

Known for its scenic hills, pleasant weather, and vibrant culture.

Mawlynnong

Asia's cleanest village and a model of sustainability.

Dawki

Famous for crystal-clear waters of the Umngot River.

Jowai

Land of beautiful caves, falls and scenic landscapes.
